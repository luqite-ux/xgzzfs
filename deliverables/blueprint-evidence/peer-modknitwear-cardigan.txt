Buttoned, zipped and open-front cardigans, long and cropped lengths, V-neck and crew-neck, in plain, rib, cable, jacquard or intarsia knits, for womenswear and menswear. How do you stop the front of a cardigan from stretching? We size the front band to the body, choose a firm stitch for the placket and, where needed, add a tape facing behind the buttons and buttonholes. Can you make two-way zip cardigans? Yes. We work with one-way and two-way zips in metal or coil, specified in your tech pack and fitted with a knitted or taped guard. Are pockets knitted in or sewn on? Both are possible. Patch pockets are knitted separately and linked or sewn on; inset pockets are knitted into the front panel. We show both on the proto so you can choose. What is the minimum order for cardigans? Our standard MOQ is 300 pieces per style and colour. For new brand partners we start from 100 pieces per style and colour. The final quantity depends on yarn, gauge and finishing.
